Dimension Type_ Code. Type/Code. Content
Full CL
CC
Code. Content
 
 
  
1
  
Gross dimensions
The dimension expressed in a gross value.
  
2
  
Package dimensions (including goods)
The dimension of the goods including the packaging.
  
3
  
Pallet dimensions (excluding goods)
The dimension of a pallet excluding the goods.
  
4
  
Pallet dimensions (including goods)
The dimension of a pallet including the goods.
  
5
  
Off-standard dimension front
The dimension in the length that the cargo exceeds the standard length at the front of an equipment.
  
6
  
Off-standard dimension back
The dimension in the length that the cargo exceeds the standard length at the back of an equipment.
  
7
  
Off-standard dimension right
The dimension in the width that the cargo exceeds the standard width at the right side of an equipment.
  
8
  
Off-standard dimension left
The dimension in the width that the cargo exceeds the standard width at the left side of an equipment.
  
9
  
Off-standard dimension general
The dimensions that the cargo exceeds the standard dimensions.
  
10
  
External equipment dimension
The external dimensions of transport equipment.
  
11
  
Internal equipment dimensions
The internal dimensions of equipment.
  
12
  
Damage dimensions
Dimensions of the damaged area.
  
13
  
Off-standard dimensions height
The dimension in the height that the cargo exceeds the standard height at the top of a piece of equipment.
  
14
  
Equipment door dimensions
Dimensions (width and height) of the equipment door.
  
15
  
Off-standard dimension width
The dimension of the width that the cargo exceeds the standard width of a piece of equipment.
  
16
  
Off-standard dimension length
The dimension of the length that the cargo exceeds the standard length of a piece of equipment.
  
17
  
Bundled equipment total height
Total height of multiple pieces of equipment transported as a single unit (e.g. bundles of folded flat racks).
  
18
  
Equipment off-standard dimension height, actual
Actual height of equipment. To be specified for containers if actual height is not defined by a specific ISO size type code.
  
19
  
Folded equipment height
Height of equipment in folded condition (e.g. flat rack with end walls folded).
  
20
  
Adjustable equipment height
Actual height of adjustable equipment (e.g. flat rack with telescopic corner posts).
  
21
  
Equipment floor height
Floor height of supporting equipment where un-containerized cargo is stowed on.
  
22
  
Container off-standard dimension width at corner posts
Container's actual width at corner post. To be specified if different than 8 feet.
  
23
  
Container off-standard dimension width of body
Container's actual width of body. To be specified if different than 8 feet.
  
24
  
Transport unit gross dimensions
The gross dimensions of a transport unit.
